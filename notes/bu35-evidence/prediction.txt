Prediction, before implementation

The date formatter should cover all documented C-locale conversions, padding,
width and case flags, epoch seconds and nanoseconds, and numeric zone forms.
I predict ISO/RFC dates, @seconds, numeric offsets, weekday and relative units
can ship; obscure free-form combinations will need explicit refusal cases.
The existing bore.clock TZif/POSIX reader should avoid a general named-zone
gap, but abbreviations and leap-second zones need separate measurement.
Clock setting and clock resolution may lack a pinned surface.

I predict at least 50 documented stty settings cannot be expressed by the
pinned term.Mode API; the exact named list must be measured, not inferred.
I predict the Ubuntu container has no live utmp login records, and GNU who
will not honour an arbitrary UTMP environment variable. An explicit FILE and
constructed 384-byte Linux records should provide deterministic comparisons.

After bu33 merges, predict 102 of 103 utility units have source entries, df
remaining absent; chroot, priority-changing nice and arbitrary-child stdbuf
remain named central-operation gaps. Recount units rather than assuming 103.
